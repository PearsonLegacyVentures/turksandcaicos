Massage for villas in Grace Bay, Leeward and Long Bay

Villa appointments should be organised around the actual property, not just an island name. Grace Bay includes resort residences and rentals; Leeward has gated and canal-side homes; Long Bay includes beach-facing villas that may need sheltered indoor treatment space. Each location has different host permission, arrival and equipment requirements.

Share your villa's name, precise location, entrance instructions and whether the owner permits an outside massage professional. An individual session needs a quiet private room; simultaneous couples massage needs adequate space for two tables and confirmed local professionals.

Services you can request

Guests can enquire about relaxation-focused Swedish massage, firmer deep tissue work and couples treatments. For a villa group, tell us the number of guests, whether you need simultaneous sessions, desired durations and any special requests. We confirm appropriately qualified local coverage, practical setup and the complete quote before the booking is accepted.

What happens before confirmation?

  1. Choose your treatment, 60 or 90 minutes, and number of guests.
  2. Provide the villa name, area and property entry conditions.
  3. Ask your host whether outside wellness providers are permitted.
  4. Share a local date and preferred appointment time.
  5. Confirm staff availability, travel requirements and the full price.

Common villa massage questions

Can a therapist visit a villa in Leeward or Long Bay?

You can submit a request. We must confirm the host's permission, a qualified local professional and any travel charges before providing a final time.

Can two couples receive massages simultaneously?

That depends on available staff and suitable room space. Give the group size and desired timetable so an appropriately staffed plan can be reviewed.
